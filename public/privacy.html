Little Loop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · Applies to the Little Loop app and the site that hosts it

The short version

Little Loop keeps your photos on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no cloud uploads. The only network requests the app makes are to load its own code and, when you export a video, to download the open-source video encoder it uses.

What Little Loop stores

  • Your timelines, photos, notes, and settings, stored locally in the app on your device.
  • Your purchase unlock, stored on-device and re-verifiable through your app store.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it yourself (for example, saving a backup file or sharing a video through your operating system's share sheet).

What Little Loop does not do

  • No sign-up, no accounts, no email addresses collected.
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or cookies for tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data — there is no personal data collected to sell or share.
  • No third-party SDKs that receive your photos or content.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by your app store (Apple App Store / Google Play) or by our payment processor on the web. We never see or store your payment details. Your unlock is tied to your app store account and can be restored on a new device from the app.

Backups and exports

Backup files and exported videos are created on your device and are yours. If you share or upload them somewhere, that destination's own privacy policy applies to what you send it.

Children's privacy

Little Loop is designed for parents and guardians capturing their own family's moments, entirely on their own device. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children, so there is nothing to comply with under COPPA/GDPR-K beyond what this policy already states: we collect nothing.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change and the update will ship with the next app release. Material changes will be called out in the app's release notes.
